Arm position (cm)

Target position of the gripper tip, measured from the point on the ground directly below the rotating arm base.

X
Side to side: positive moves right, negative moves left.
Y
Forward reach: positive moves away from the base.
Z
Height above the ground-origin plane: positive moves up.
Pitch
Gripper angle in degrees: 0° is level, positive tilts up, negative tilts down.
Seconds
Time allowed for the arm to complete the movement.
